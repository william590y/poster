Limitations: the authors' and ours

Authors state

“Initial proof-of-concept”; “models below 25M parameters at a 4K context” (pp.1, 15)

Whether discovered math “causally contribute[s] to transfer” is untested (p.15)

“Limited leakage through hyperparameter selection” on DCLM + DNA (p.8)

Our reading · calculations marked

No trivial baselines: no unigram, n-gram, PPM or copy-last-byte; only random bytes (8.02) and i.i.d. uniform programs (Table 5)

Fit reporting: frontier picked by validation loss on the reported datasets; Table 2 gives b only, no E, A, CIs or held-out extrapolation (pp.8, 21)

Reward vs pool: breaking the reward–program link keeps 74% of the DCLM gap (our calc; 27–93% over 9 datasets, Table 5); mutation, replay, expert iteration, archive never ablated
