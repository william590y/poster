About 1.5 minutes. Slide content with sources. Generator g_φ: Llama decoder, random init; samples over 19 logits until F (Sec. 2.3, p.7). Prior g_0(x) = |A|^(-ℓ(x)), the "fixed uniform program prior" (p.6); |A| = 19 is my reading (App. E's base alphabet has 9). Objective (Eq. 3): J(φ) = E_{x~g_φ}[r(x)] - β * KL(g_φ || g_0); GRPO-style advantage over the round pool, plus expert iteration (Eqs. 4-5, p.6). Learner π_θ: same Llama architecture, independent init; byte vocabulary of 256 (Sec. 2.3). Next-byte cross-entropy on outputs y_i; per-program loss = mean over "content tokens". Pool loss (Eq. 1): (1/M_e) sum_{i in B_e} L(y_i; θ), equal weight per program; fresh, mutated and replay programs all train it (p.5). Tuned per scale by coordinate descent: learner LR, generator-to-learner LR ratio, batch size, β (p.8); values not reported. Detail: Advantage (p.6): A_i = (r_i - mean_e r)/(std_e r + eps) - β*(log g_φ(x_i) - log g_0(x_i)), normalized over the full round pool. Eq. 4: policy gradient with a sequence-level importance ratio clipped to [e^-20, e^20]. It is 1 for fresh samples; for replay, the denominator is the probability stored at bank entry. Mutants are excluded from L_PG because they "were not sampled from a proposal distribution with a well-defined log probability". They still enter L_EI. Expert iteration: weights w_i = [r_i]+ / sum [r_j]+ over the whole pool, λ_EI = 1.0 (Eq. 5). [mine] [.]+ is a no-op since r ≥ 0. Generator "initialized near g_0 and regularized toward it throughout" (p.6). "Content tokens" is undefined: prefixes and zero padding may or may not count. Sizes: 1M (Table 5, Fig. 3), 24.4M (App. A.1), "below 25M" (p.15). The equations on the slide are typed from Eqs. 1 and 3; paper crops: figs/eq3-rl-objective.png, figs/eq-advantage.png.
