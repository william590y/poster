Later checkpoints: more structure, transfer flattens

Paper's Fig. 3, p.10

Setup (p.9): for endpoint T, a corpus of 4.19M programs sampled across 16 generator snapshots ending at T; a fresh 1M-param learner trains one epoch on each

Paper: “Both improve steadily with T” (p.9)

Epiplexity = “the excess training loss the learner accumulates before converging” (Fig. 3 caption); units nats × rounds

(a) Epiplexity rises: ~15 at g0 → ~1,700 at g4,096 (mean of 8); ~1,240 without the off-scale 5,001 seed (plot read, our calc)

(b) Val BPB falls, then flattens: text ~8.4 → ~5.2 by g1,024, ~5.3 at g4,096; audio ~5.4 → 5.2 after g512; images still falling slowly (~6.8 → ~6.3) (plot reads)
