Method

Generator writes programs, learner predicts bytes

Paper's Fig. 1 (top), p.3

1 · Generator, a random-init Llama, writes program x_i, ending in F

2 · Machine U runs it on random tape ω_i → bytes y_i = U(x_i, ω_i)

3 · Learner (same architecture, independent weights) trains on the bytes, not the programs (Eq. 1)

4 · Reward: the learner's gradients score each program (Eq. 2) → policy gradient + reward-weighted SFT

No natural data in any gradient step; only val-loss selection (p.8) and zero-shot eval

About 1.5 minutes. Walk the loop once (Sec. 2 items 1-3, p.4). Both networks start random: "tabula rasa" (p.2). Two random-init Llama decoders, same architecture, independent weights (pp.4, 7). Generator g_φ samples programs; machine U runs them into bytes; learner π_θ predicts the bytes (Sec. 2, p.4). The learner trains on the output bytes, not on the programs (Sec. 2.1, p.4): "These output bytes, rather than the programs themselves, constitute the learner's training data" (p.4). The learner's own gradients score each program; that score is the generator's reward. Generator update: policy gradient on the reward, plus reward-weighted SFT over a pool of fresh, replayed and mutated programs (p.4; Eq. 5). Sec. 2 item 3 also gives the generator "a supervised fine-tuning objective on existing programs to mitigate catastrophic forgetting and on mutated programs to promote exploration" (p.4). In the loop, no natural data in any gradient step; natural data enters only through validation-based selection (p.8) and zero-shot evaluation. Natural data touches the self-play pipeline only through selection: DCLM + DNA validation loss picks hyperparameters, and each dataset's validation loss picks checkpoints, ensemble sizes and frontier points (p.8). Plus zero-shot evaluation. Downstream, the final self-play checkpoint also initializes natural-data pretraining (App. A.1). App. A.1 is separate: there, self-play checkpoints are warm starts that do train on natural data. Labels from my own sketch of the loop (adapted from Fig. 1 top, p.3; arrows and labels are mine), to say while pointing at the paper's unmodified figure: the generator draws on a pool of fresh + mutated + replay programs; program x_i has variable length ≤ L and ends in F (sampled over 19 logits); the machine U is Brainf*ck + 10 macros and reads a random tape ω_i; bytes y_i = U(x_i, ω_i); the learner's loss is next-byte cross-entropy (Eq. 1); the return path is reward r_i (Eq. 2) → policy gradient + reward-weighted SFT (Eqs. 3-5); natural data gets no gradient steps, only zero-shot eval and val loss for hyperparameters, checkpoints and frontier (p.8). 19 is the logit set (8 + 10 + F, p.7), not program length; programs are in A^{≤L} (p.4), and L is not reported.
