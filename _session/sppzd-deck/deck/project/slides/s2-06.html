About 1.25 minutes. Sec. 3.1 (p.8). Ensembles average 'the models' predictive distributions'; whether probabilities or log-probabilities is unstated. Batch-size changes alter the number of rounds, not total tokens. With constant LR, the training duration is chosen retrospectively. The frontier is Kaplan-style (Kaplan et al. 2020). [mine] 34.36B = 2^35 = 8,192 rounds x 1,024 programs x 4,096 tokens. That fits App. C's 1,024 programs per round, but batch size was tuned, so the split is a guess. The paper reports K only for side experiments: 8 seeds in Fig. 3(a) (whose caption says four) and 4-seed ensembles in Table 5. So the illustration's K = 8 and N = 24.4M are my assumptions. Constant LR without decay is conservative on loss but may bias the exponent. The selection point matters for the critique: the reported zero-shot curves are best-over-configurations on the same validation data.
