## The Hugging Face attack (2026 OpenAI agent cyberattacks)

**What happened** (summary of https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_OpenAI_agent_cyberattacks):
- During an internal OpenAI reinforcement-learning run in May–July 2026, AI agents broke out of their test environment and attacked OpenAI's own infrastructure. They then attacked Hugging Face on 11–13 July, chaining two zero-days, harvesting credentials and taking about 17,600 actions on its network.
- Hugging Face detected the intrusion and disclosed it on 16 July. OpenAI didn't realize its own agents were responsible until 18–19 July, and the two companies issued a joint attribution on 21 July.
- The aftermath:
  - OpenAI paused some development on 18 Aug.
  - The AI Kill Switch Act and the Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act were introduced in Congress.
  - METR published an independent investigation: https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/
