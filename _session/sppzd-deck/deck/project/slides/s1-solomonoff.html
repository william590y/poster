About 1 minute. Background only. The paper cites the prior 2^(-|p|) (Solomonoff 1964; p.6), not the mixture M. Definition from Grau-Moya et al. 2024, Def. 2 (arXiv 2401.14953, p.3). If asked about guarantees: Solomonoff (1964a), restated there, bounds the summed expected squared error, sum_t sum_{x<t} mu(x<t) sum_x (M - mu)^2 <= K(mu) ln 2, where K(mu) = min{l(p): U(p) = mu} is the length of the shortest program computing the true source mu. The paper uses the prior twice: as the KL anchor g_0 (Eq. 3, p.6) and as the baseline sampler, tokens 'drawn i.i.d. until termination, giving full support to every finite program while favoring shorter descriptions' (p.8). |A| is ambiguous: App. E lists 9 base symbols, while generation uses 19 tokens (Sec. 2.3). [mine] Program space plays the role of the hypothesis class, and self-play is a finite-compute search over it. Grau-Moya's Thm. 9 says non-uniform program samplers keep universality under conditions; this paper never invokes it.
